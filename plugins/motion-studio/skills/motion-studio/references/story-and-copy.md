# Story and copy

## Discovery (before any frame)

1. Write the single question the film answers, in the viewer's words ("Why do my gutters keep overflowing?"), not the company's.
2. List every fact you could use, then split it: **must-know** (the film fails without it) and **cut** (true, interesting, gone). The cut list stays cut; don't sneak items back in as captions.
3. Find the hook: the belief most viewers hold that turns out to be wrong ("clean gutters in October means clean in December"). The film exists to flip it.
4. Use a metaphor only if it makes the idea *more accurate*. If the real thing can be shown (a cutaway gutter, a meter filling), show the real thing.
5. Every number needs a source in `SOURCES.md`: a URL with the date read, or, when the brief supplies the fact, "brief: <who>, <date>" with their exact wording. No source, no number. Prefer qualitative labels ("FASCIA: SATURATED") over invented readings.

## Arcs

Pick the arc from the brief. Percentages are of total runtime.

**Explainer arc** (teaching, calm, sound-on or off):

| Beat | Share | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Question | 0-10% | Open on the misconception as an *image*, never a title card |
| Model | 10-35% | The smallest correct picture of how it works; one element per beat |
| Proof | 35-75% | Run the model on a real case; cause and effect play out on screen |
| Turn | 75-90% | Change one variable, show what follows; this is where it clicks |
| Payoff | 90-100% | The opening image again, now read correctly, plus one next step |

**Social ad arc** (stop the scroll, keep the watch, earn the call):

| Beat | Time (30 s) | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0-2 s | The climax first, mid-motion, text on frame 1 ("ONE LEAF DID THIS.") |
| Smash cut | ~2 s | "60 DAYS EARLIER" style time jump: opens a curiosity loop |
| Build | 2-15 s | Escalating chain of causes, a visible meter or counter that keeps climbing |
| Loop closes | ~12 s | The cold-open moment happens again in context; viewer's question answered |
| Turn | 15-25 s | Rewind or reset; the fix in 3-4 named, visible steps |
| Proof | ~24 s | Same conditions, different result (split screen WITHOUT / WITH works) |
| Offer | last 3-4 s | Brand on its correct background, one offer, phone, URL |

Both arcs: the last image answers the first one.

## Scene spec (write one per scene before animating)

```
id / time range:   S3  7.2-10.2s
teaches:           the one new thing this scene adds
frame:             what is on screen, in order of importance (1 focal element)
motion:            enter -> key action -> settle -> exit
words:             on-screen text (<= 8 words a line, <= 2 lines); voice line if any
sound:             the cue that marks the key action (thunder on the crack, click on the snap)
check:             what the viewer can now say that they couldn't before
```

If two scenes have the same "teaches", merge them. If "check" is vague, the scene has no job; cut it.

## Copy rules

- Two lines on screen, maximum. Plain words before jargon; if a term is needed, show the thing as you name it.
- On-screen words are anchors, not the script. Never put the whole voice line on screen; put the 2-4 words it hinges on.
- One name per thing, used every time (pick "fascia" and never switch to "trim board").
- Every number traces to `SOURCES.md`, or it goes.
- Claims about a company come only from its own pages or the owner. Offers (free inspection, warranty years) must appear on the company site.
- Run captions and VO lines through a slop check (the stop-slop / unslop skill if available): no "not just X", no stacked triads, no announcer phrases.
